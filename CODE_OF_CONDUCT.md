# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. This is a small hobby project; nobody is being paid to
tolerate rudeness.

## What that means

- Assume good faith. People asking "obvious" questions were beginners once, and
  so were you.
- Criticise code, not people. "This breaks on 640×384 panels" is useful;
  "this is amateur work" is not.
- Harassment, personal attacks, and discrimination on any grounds are not
  tolerated, in issues, pull requests, commit messages, or anywhere else the
  project is discussed.
- Respect a maintainer's "no". A rejected feature is not a judgement of you,
  and the project's scope is deliberately narrow.

## Enforcement

Report problems by email to the maintainer listed in [LICENSE](LICENSE), or
privately via GitHub. Reports stay confidential.

Responses escalate as needed: a warning, having a comment edited or removed, or
being blocked from the repository. Maintainers who behave badly are held to the
same standard, and there is no appeal to seniority here.
