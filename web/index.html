Let's get your display online
Welcome 👋

This wizard runs on the display itself. In the next few steps it will:

  • join your home WiFi,
  • connect to your email (IMAP — works with Migadu, Fastmail, and most providers),
  • find your calendar (CalDAV auto-discovery, or an ICS link),
  • set your weather location, clock and refresh schedule.

Each step is tested live before you move on, so problems show up here — not as a blank screen later. Nothing leaves the device: credentials are stored only in its internal flash.

1 · WiFi

Pick your home network (2.4 GHz only — the ESP32 can't see 5 GHz networks).

While the device joins your network its setup hotspot may drop for ~10 seconds. Stay connected to the setup WiFi — this page will recover by itself.
2 · Email (IMAP)
Advanced (host, port, folder)
You can also skip email: leave the address empty and press Next.
3 · Calendar
Migadu: https://cdav.migadu.com/ · Fastmail: https://caldav.fastmail.com/ · Nextcloud: https://cloud.example.com/remote.php/dav/
Google Calendar: Settings → your calendar → "Secret address in iCal format".

5 · Clock & refresh

6 · Review & finish
The device reboots, this setup network disappears, and the first dashboard appears on the e-paper within about a minute.
✅
All set!

Configuration saved. The display is rebooting and will draw your dashboard shortly.
You can close this page and rejoin your normal WiFi.

To change settings later: tap RST, then immediately press & hold BOOT for ~2 seconds — the setup network will come back. (Don't hold BOOT while pressing RST: that puts the chip into its flashing mode instead.)
